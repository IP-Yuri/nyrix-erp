## 📋 PREAMBLE & COMMERCIAL INTENT

This **Turnkey Software Acquisition and Source Code Handover Agreement** establishes the terms under which **Salaheddine CHIKHI / AVYR Digital** sells, delivers, and transfers the **NYRIX ERP Core System** and all associated source code to the purchasing entity (the "Client").

This Agreement is specifically structured to achieve two reciprocal objectives:
1. **Total Operational Autonomy for the Client:** The Client acquires the software with complete source code delivery, enjoying perpetual, irrevocable rights to deploy, host, run, modify, and extend the system internally without recurring software license fees or third-party dependency.
2. **Pedigree Attribution & Agency Asset Preservation for AVYR Digital:** The Client permanently preserves the Developer's authorship attribution ("Powered by AVYR Digital") across the platform, and the Developer retains full ownership of its underlying technical components, architectural patterns, and reusable code libraries (Background IP) for deployment across other non-competing projects.

---

## 1. 📦 DELIVERY & FULL SOURCE CODE TRANSFER

1. **Complete Codebase Handover:**  
   The Developer delivers the Software in full, human-readable, uncompiled source code, including:
   * Backend services (FastAPI REST API, relational data models, business logic engines).
   * Relational database architectures (PostgreSQL schemas, migration patterns, and indexes).
   * Complete frontend modules (HTML5, JavaScript ES6+, styling assets, and icons).
   * External integrations (courier webhooks, Google Sheets live matchmaker, parcel tracking).
   * Comprehensive operational documentation, seed scripts, and automated test suites.

2. **Zero Cloud Lock-in & No Recurring License Fees:**  
   The acquisition is a one-time turnkey purchase. The Developer imposes no recurring software license seat fees, no remote activation checks, no telemetry trackers, and no remote disabling mechanisms ("kill-switches").

---

## 2. 🏛️ RIGHTS CONFERRED TO THE CLIENT

Subject to full payment of the agreed commercial acquisition fee:

1. **Perpetual & Irrevocable Usage Rights:**  
   The Developer grants to the Client an exclusive-in-business, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free right to:
   * Install, run, and self-host the Software on any cloud, private virtual server, container cluster, or local on-premise warehouse hardware owned or leased by the Client.
   * Utilize the Software without limitation for all internal business operations, logistics fulfillment, warehouse intake, courier tracking, and commercial B2B sales.
   * Create unlimited user accounts, stations, and tablet endpoints without incurring per-seat charges.

2. **Unrestricted Modification & Maintenance Freedom:**  
   The Client is granted the full right to adapt, configure, customize, maintain, update, and enhance the source code—either via the Client's internal engineering team or through third-party technical contractors of the Client's choosing—without requiring approval from the Developer.

3. **Commercial Scope & Prohibited Exploitation:**  
   The Client purchases the Software for its own corporate and operational benefit. The Client shall **NOT** package, resell, sublicense, rent, or white-label the Software as an independent off-the-shelf commercial SaaS product to external third parties without a separate co-commercialization agreement with AVYR Digital.

## 4. ⚙️ BACKGROUND IP & DEVELOPER REUSE RIGHTS (TRONC COMMUN)

To ensure that the Developer's agency is not stripped of its core technical assets and engineering foundation:

1. **Partition of Intellectual Property:**
   * **Client Specific IP (*Développements Spécifiques*):** The Client's business identity, trademarks, logos, custom pricing formulas, proprietary supplier directories, and trade secrets configured within the system belong exclusively to the Client.
   * **Agency Background IP (*Tronc Commun & Savoir-Faire Préexistant*):** The underlying framework architectures, generic database schema designs, authentication/session management logic, idempotent inventory deduction mechanisms, courier webhook receivers, Google Sheets synchronization logic, and general software libraries represent the Developer's pre-existing technical capital ("Background IP").

2. **Developer's Unrestricted Right of Reuse:**  
   **AVYR Digital explicitly retains the unrestricted right to reuse, adapt, expand, refine, and deploy the Background IP, code modules, algorithmic concepts, and general architectural frameworks for other past, present, or future clients, projects, and industries.**

3. **Protection Against Derivative Monopolization:**  
   Nothing in this Agreement shall be interpreted as preventing AVYR Digital from developing other ERP, logistics, landed-cost, or warehouse management solutions for other entities, provided no confidential business records or proprietary Client trademarks are disclosed.

## 5. 🤝 CLIENT MARKET EXCLUSIVITY & COMPETITIVE PROTECTION

In consideration of the Client's acquisition investment:

1. **Non-Duplication for Direct Competitors:**  
   The Developer formally commits not to sell, license, or deploy the exact operational turnkey configuration, including the Client's customized landed-cost modeling and tailored B2B workflow adaptations, to any known direct commercial competitor operating within the Client's specific retail market niche in Morocco for a period of twelve (12) months following handover.

2. **Data Confidentiality:**  
   The Developer shall maintain strict confidentiality over all client business records, turnover metrics, margins, and supplier agreements observed during development.
